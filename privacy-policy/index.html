Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The short version

  • We only use analytics on our Startup Idea Session page, and only if you click Accept.
  • Analytics never collects your name, phone number, email or anything you type.
  • We do not use advertising tracking or ad personalisation.
  • You can change your choice at any time.

Who we are

YoungMinds.app is run by YM Innovations Pvt Ltd (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information our website, www.youngminds.app, collects when you visit it and how we use it.

Analytics on the Startup Idea Session page

Only with your permission

We use Google Analytics 4, loaded through Google Tag Manager, on our Startup Idea Session page (youngminds.app/startup-idea-session/) only. The rest of youngminds.app does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

The first time you visit that page, a banner asks whether you accept cookies. Nothing is loaded and no analytics cookie is set unless you click Accept. If you click Decline, or don't answer, the page works exactly the same and nothing is sent to Google Analytics.

What we measure

If you accept, we measure how the page is used, for example:

  • which parts of the page were viewed and how far people scrolled
  • which FAQ questions were opened, and which “Read more” links on reviews were used
  • whether a student video was played, played halfway, or finished
  • clicks on the “Book” buttons and the WhatsApp link

Google Analytics also receives the general information it collects from any website. This includes your device and browser type, approximate location (country or city), and how you found the page.

What we do not collect

Analytics never collects your name, phone number, email address or anything you type. A click on a “Book” button only tells us that someone clicked it, not who booked or paid.

Settings we use

  • Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses.
  • Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, so this data is not used to build advertising profiles.
  • We do not use Meta (Facebook or Instagram) tracking on our website.

Services we use

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (Google LLC): page analytics, as described above, only after you accept. Google may process this data outside India.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load their font from Google, which receives your IP address to deliver it. No cookies are set.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this website. Like any web host, it may log technical information such as IP addresses for security.
  • Wise.live: bookings and payments for our sessions happen on Wise.live, not on our website. Wise.live's own privacy policy applies there.
  • WhatsApp: if you message us on WhatsApp, we receive your phone number and the messages you send, and use them to reply to you. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that service.

How long we keep analytics data

Google Analytics keeps detailed event data for 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.

Students under 18

Many visitors to our website are students aged 13 to 18. That is why analytics stays off unless someone clicks Accept, why we never collect names or contact details through analytics, and why we do not use advertising tracking or ad personalisation. If you are under 18, we encourage you to decide about cookies together with a parent or guardian.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your cookie choice: click Cookie settings at the bottom of the Startup Idea Session page.
  • Remove cookies: clear the cookies and site data for youngminds.app in your browser settings.
  • Ask about your data: you can contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, or to raise a concern.

Contact us

Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date at the top.
